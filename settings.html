Uplink Settings

Pick the AI that writes your notes, where they are saved, and which of your agents learn from them.

1. Your AI

Apps on this computer use your existing sign-in. API keys stay in a private file on this computer.

2. Notes library

Every lesson is saved here as a text file, with its transcript and screenshots.

3. Your shortcuts

Click a box, then press the keys you want. Use at least one of Control, Option (Alt) or Command (Windows key) with a letter, number, F-key or Space.

4. Who learns from your notes

Each lesson goes to the agents it fits. Ticked agents get a reading list and a line in their instructions to read it before a job.

Your own (Custom GPTs, Gems, Claude Projects)

Each gets its own upload folder. Add new notes from it to the GPT, Gem or Project's files.

Nothing ticked: notes are filed by topic (design, video, marketing and so on).

5. Tell your AI about the library

Adds one short paragraph to your AI app's main instructions, so every chat knows where your notes are.
